About the game

BeatBounds was our major project in the final year of university, made with one other teammate. It is a rhythm game: what the player is allowed to do is driven entirely by the music. Each track hands the player its own set of movement and action options, and those rules have to be followed in time with the beat — with visual cues marking the moment the music changes.

Tilesets

Ground Tiles

Forest, cave and mountain tilesets, six tiles each — a top edge, its two corners, a slope either side and a plain fill — plus the poison water surface. Tiles are the one asset where a mistake repeats itself across a whole level, so they are shown assembled rather than piece by piece, which is the only way the joins can actually be judged.

Story

Narrative

The player character guards time — every alternative timeline and world, watched from a room of monitors. While he is on duty something breaks in the main timeline, the alarm goes up, and he goes out through the gates to find it: up the moonlit stairs to the great clock that keeps time for every world. He finds it broken. That is where the game begins, and why he is running through the levels at all — he is looking for the key to put it right.

Six panels carry that, so the game never has to stop and explain itself.
