Character Portraits

Real model photography, turned into game character figures. Every character was cut three ways — full body, torso-up and face — across five poses to start, with more poses and more characters added later: somewhere north of three hundred images in all. The thirty here are the torso-up set, held to one framing and lighting spec so the roster reads as a single cast.

Edited every figure myself from supplied photography, to a brief that set the effects and treatments.
