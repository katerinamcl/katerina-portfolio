About the game

Kryat was a second-year university project, made as part of a team. It is an adventure card game: players capture creatures to gain dice, and those dice are what they use to battle the grand beasts.

I was the team’s art director and made all of the artwork in the game — this page collects that work. By the end we produced a physical deck of cards, every one of them printed with these designs.
